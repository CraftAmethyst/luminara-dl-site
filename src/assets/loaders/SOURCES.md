# Loader icons

All icons are SVG with transparent backgrounds.

- Forge: official anvil SVG from
  <https://files.minecraftforge.net/static/images/safari-pinned-tab.svg>
  (retrieved 2026-10-18).
- NeoForge: vectorized from the official favicon
  <https://neoforged.net/favicon.ico> (retrieved 2026-10-18). The gray tile
  background was removed; the fox head is preserved as vector paths.
- Fabric: vectorized from the official favicon
  <https://fabricmc.net/assets/favicon.png> (retrieved 2026-10-18).

Forge and Fabric/NeoForge marks belong to their respective projects; they are
used here only to identify the loader.
